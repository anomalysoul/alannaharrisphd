About

Anomaly.

I've spent over a decade doing this work for real. K–12 classrooms, university lecture halls, federal grant projects, district coaching, national conferences. My Ph.D. in Urban Education from UW-Milwaukee gave me the language for what I was already doing. The work came first.

I know how to walk into a room full of skeptical teachers and leave with them actually wanting to change something. I know how to read data and turn it into a plan people will actually follow. I know how to write, present, coach, facilitate, and lead without losing the people I'm supposed to be serving.

And yes, I DJ. Because teaching and DJing are the same thing: read the room, hold the energy, know when to shift the vibe. I bring that same skill everywhere I go.
