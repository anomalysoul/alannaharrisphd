Weekly Outreach Schedule

The scheduler automatically sends outreach emails to professors every week on the schedule configured in your .env file (OUTREACH_CRON). Default: Monday at 9:00 AM CT.

Professors are contacted no more than once every 7 days (OUTREACH_INTERVAL_DAYS). The batch is capped at 10 emails per run (OUTREACH_BATCH_SIZE).

Google Sheets

Data is stored in your Google Sheet configured via GOOGLE_SHEETS_ID in the .env file. The app manages four tabs: Applications, Professors, Email Log, and Templates.
